# 1. THE BRAND STORY

## What The Torts Attorney actually is

We are a legal marketing and case-acquisition services company built for
plaintiff law firms.

We help firms build and operate the path from **first response to
qualified case opportunity**.

That can include:

**Campaign strategy → media → landing experience → consent capture →
intake → qualification → follow-up → documentation → retainer workflow →
delivery → reporting**

We are not presenting ourselves as a law firm.

We are presenting ourselves as the **operating layer that helps
plaintiff firms acquire, qualify and understand demand.**

## Core brand promise

**Build a plaintiff pipeline your team can actually work. --- from first
click to qualified case opportunity.**

## Brand tagline

**Where Better Cases Begin With Better Acquisition.**

## Supporting line

**Campaign strategy, intake operations, qualification workflows and
transparent reporting for plaintiff law firms.**

## The human idea behind the brand

A lead is not the finish line.

A person has to trust the page enough to respond.\
The response has to become a usable record.\
The record has to be contacted.\
The inquiry has to be qualified against the right criteria.\
The right information has to be collected.\
The opportunity has to reach the right firm.\
And the firm needs to understand what happened afterward.

**We build the system around that entire journey.**

# 6. HOMEPAGE --- PROBLEM SECTION

## Eyebrow

**THE FORM IS ONLY THE BEGINNING**

## H2

**A lead is only valuable if the next steps hold up.**

## Body

A campaign can generate responses and still underperform.

The real questions begin after the form is submitted:

Was the prospect contacted quickly?\
Did the intake team ask the right questions?\
Was the record actually qualified?\
Were missing documents followed up on?\
Was the status recorded correctly?\
Did the opportunity reach the right law firm?\
And can anyone see which source ultimately produced the strongest
opportunities?

**That is where we put our attention: what happens after the response.**

## Problem card 1

### The first response matters

Interest fades quickly when a prospect submits an inquiry and hears
nothing back.

**We build contact cadence and escalation around the campaign rather
than leaving follow-up to chance.**

## Problem card 2

### A generic script misses the point

A generic script can create unnecessary fallout and inconsistent
decisions.

**We translate firm-approved criteria into campaign-specific screening
and disposition logic.**

## Problem card 3

### When the numbers stop telling the story

If media, intake and delivery live in separate systems, it becomes
difficult to understand what is actually working.

**We connect source, status, qualification and delivery information so
the pipeline can be understood.**

## Problem card 4

### More traffic is not always the answer

More traffic does not help if intake capacity, documentation or firm
review cannot keep up.

**We plan acquisition against the people, process and capacity that have
to handle it.**
